MindfulFade 🌙 | AI Helper for Digital Wellbeing

A simple AI-powered interactive concept designed for Grade 11 ICT students to tackle late-night screen time habits. 

---

## 📝 Assignment Deliverables

### 1. Problem
Grade 11 students frequently lose crucial hours of sleep because they get stuck in endless, habit-forming scrolling loops on social media late at night.

### 2. AI Idea
**MindfulFade** — An AI-driven companion extension that actively analyzes late-night interaction behavior to gently push users off their phones without frustrating blocklists.

### 3. Core Features / Tips
* **Dynamic Grayscale Fading:** Automatically shifts the screen completely to grayscale and lowers intensity over 15 minutes when repetitive scrolling is detected past bedtime.
* **Interactive Breathing Overlays:** Displays transparent, calming 5-second mindfulness breathing bubbles directly over feeds rather than hard-locking applications.
* **Positive Morning Metrics:** Offers a quick, encouraging notification the next morning highlighting how many minutes of deep sleep were protected.

### 4. Why is it useful?
Our AI helper solves **late-night sleep deprivation** by **gently breaking the psychological scrolling loop using smart visual cues instead of frustrating hard restrictions.**
