MindfulFade

AI Topic: Screen Time

1. Problem

Grade 11 students frequently lose crucial hours of sleep because they get stuck in endless, habit-forming scrolling loops on social media late at night.

2. AI Idea

An AI-driven companion tool that tracks late-night scrolling patterns and subtly fades device visual stimulation to break the scrolling loop organically.

3. Core Features & Tips

Dynamic Grayscale

Slowly drains color and lowers brightness over 15 minutes upon detecting habitual loops.

Breathing Overlays

Interjects transparent, calm breathing prompts instead of using frustrating rigid app locks.

Morning Metrics

Sends encouraging morning updates showing how much sleep quality was recovered.

4. Why is it useful?
Our AI helper solves late-night sleep deprivation by gently breaking the psychological scrolling loop using smart visual cues instead of frustrating hard restrictions.
